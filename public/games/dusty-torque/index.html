VEHICLE ACCESSORIES

Free to play. Built for the long way.

Drive & jump

Hold ↑ to accelerate and ↓ to brake. Use ← and → to tilt in the air. Press Space or tap JUMP to hop; press P to pause. Touch players can hold the pedals below the game.

Stick the landing

Leave Auto Level on while learning. Look for yellow jump signs and ease off the gas if a landing looks steep. Clean landings earn airtime bonuses.

Build your garage

Collect coins and complete stages to unlock vehicles, improve engine power and fuel duration, and equip cosmetic accessories. Everything uses earned game coins.

Explore seven tracks

Each track generates a fresh route, starts gently, and saves its own stage. Completing a stage unlocks a longer, tougher run. Your progress stays in this browser and on this web address.
