Your game. Your progress.

Privacy information · Updated November 10, 2026

Dusty Torque is a free browser driving game published by GRZ News. This page describes the current game and its planned advertising setup.

Saved game data

The game stores coins, vehicle ownership, upgrades, equipped accessories, selected tracks, best distance and track stages in your browser’s local storage. The storage key is dustTorque. The game does not send this saved progress to a game server. Saves are specific to your browser and this website address; they are not synchronized across devices or domains.

Deleting progress

You can delete the game’s saved data by clearing this site’s storage in your browser settings. This removes locally saved progress and purchases made with earned game coins. Those game purchases are not real-money payments.

Advertising status

Third-party advertising is currently disabled. The game’s advertising module does not load Google’s advertising script or request ads in this release. There are no paid downloads, real-money game purchases or rewarded-ad offers in the current game.

If advertising is activated later, this notice will be updated with the providers used, the data they process and available privacy choices before ads are enabled. Google advertising can involve cookies or similar technologies and processing of information such as device/browser details, IP address and ad interactions. Any required consent messages will be handled through the configured consent management platform.

Hosting and external services

Your browser connects to the website host to download the game. The hosting service may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, browser information and requested pages, for delivery and security. This game does not add an analytics tracker or ask you to create a player account.

Changes

This page will be updated when the game’s data practices change. Advertising activation and any new player-account features will require an updated notice.
